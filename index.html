How it works

Each aspiration is the sum of three interests. Knowledge: environment, paranormal, work. Family: school, toys, animals. Popularity: weather, sports, culture. Romance: fashion, travel, health. Pleasure: food, entertainment, sci-fi. Fortune: politics, money, crime. Grilled Cheese: food, entertainment, toys.

If the highest score belongs to your Sim's primary aspiration, the next highest is used instead. On a tie, pick a number from 1 to 100: with two tied aspirations, 1–50 picks the first and 51–100 the second; with three, the ranges are 1–33, 34–66 and 67–100.
